Logo Guidelines

The MK mark is a geometric monogram built on precise angular structure — reflecting clarity, intelligence, and a system-led design philosophy.

Logo Variants
Madhan Kannan
Full Lockup — Light Surface
Madhan Kannan
Full Lockup — Sage Background
Clear Space & Minimum Size
Clear space = the x-height of the mark on all sides
Never crowd the logo with other elements
48px — Digital minimum
32px — Favicon / App icon
Do's & Don'ts
Do
Use on brand-approved backgrounds (mint, sage surface, white card)
Maintain minimum clear space on all sides at all times
Use full lockup when space allows; logomark alone for icon contexts
Scale proportionally — never alter aspect ratio
Don't
Stretch, rotate, skew, or distort the mark in any way
Apply drop shadows, outlines, or decorative effects
Change logo colors outside of approved brand variants
Place on busy, low-contrast, or off-brand backgrounds
