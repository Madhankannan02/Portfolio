Logo Guidelines

The MK mark is a minimal, geometric monogram built on precise structure. It reflects clarity, intelligence, and a system-oriented design philosophy.

Logo Variants

of logo mark size

Do's & Don'ts
Do
Use the logo on brand-approved backgrounds only (mint, white, sage)
Maintain minimum clear space on all sides
Use the full lockup when space allows
Scale the logo proportionally at all times
Use the logomark alone for icon/app contexts
Don't
Stretch, rotate, or skew the logo in any way
Apply drop shadows or decorative effects
Change logo colors outside of approved variants
Place the logo on busy or low-contrast backgrounds
Recreate the logo using different typefaces or proportions
04

Brand Voice

The voice of this brand is considered and deliberate. It never performs urgency or enthusiasm. It earns attention through precision and relevance.

Voice Pillars
⬡
Clear & Precise
Every word earns its place. No filler, no hedging — only calibrated communication.
◎
Calm & Confident
Authority that doesn't need to announce itself. Quiet certainty, not loud assertion.
⊞
System-Oriented
Thinks in structures, patterns, and dependencies. Connects parts to the whole.
—
Minimal & Intentional
Says more by saying less. Respects the reader's intelligence and time.
Writing Examples — Do vs Don't
Write this
I design systems that work — at the intersection of strategy, interface, and intelligence.
Each engagement begins with clarity on the problem. Then we build toward precision.
AI doesn't replace judgment. It extends it — if you design it well.
Not this
I'm super passionate about design and love creating amazing digital experiences!!!
Let's hop on a call and brainstorm synergies around your brand journey together!
AI is literally transforming everything and I'm obsessed with disrupting the space!
